For Corporate Partners

Invest in workforce readiness.

Partners help fund seats, host real project challenges, and gain early visibility into AI-ready talent.

  • Early Access: Gain a direct connection to top-tier technical graduates.

Why apply / why invest

The value is not just exposure to AI. It is a stronger path from curiosity to capability.

For participants, that means a more credible learning experience. For partners, that means a clearer way to invest in future talent, opportunity, and practical workforce relevance.
