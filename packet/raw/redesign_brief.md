# Redesign Brief: WrightNow Solutions

## What to Preserve
- Keep the strongest current positioning idea, but rewrite it into a tighter conversion-first hero.
- Reuse the parts of the existing site structure that align with actual service intent.

## What to Improve
- Create a clearer primary CTA system with one dominant action repeated throughout the site.
- Add a credibility rail for certifications, badges, partner logos, or guarantees.
- Add a cleaner estimate/contact capture experience with lower friction.

## Content Gaps
- Partner and certification proof appears missing or understated.

## UX / Conversion Gaps
- Current CTA repetition is weak or inconsistent.
- Lead capture looks underpowered because a visible form flow was not detected.

## Suggested Site Map
- Home
- Services
- Reviews
- About
- Gallery
- Contact

## Suggested Homepage Section Order
- Hero with primary CTA
- Trust bar / proof strip
- Core services overview
- Why choose us
- Service area coverage
- Testimonials / review themes
- Gallery or project proof
- Lead capture section

## Visual Direction
- Modernize the current brand while keeping familiar trust cues.

## Messaging Improvements
- Tighten the value proposition around speed, trust, and service quality.
- Replace vague claims with specific proof, guarantees, and local relevance.
- Use customer language from repeated review themes instead of generic filler.

## Asset Reuse Plan
- Review current site imagery for reusable logo, crew, truck, project, and hero assets.

## Missing Assets
- No missing assets flagged.

## Credibility Strategy
- Review proof
- Service-area specificity
- Visible contact methods

## Lead Capture Strategy
- Primary call CTA in hero and sticky header
- Secondary estimate form on homepage and contact page
- Repeat CTA after services, proof, and service-area sections
