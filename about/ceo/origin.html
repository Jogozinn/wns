The Origin Story

A journey from technical systems to people systems.

The foundation of WrightNow Solutions wasn't built in a classroom; it was forged in the high-stakes environment of global IT portfolio management.

Return to CEO Profile

The Corporate Realization

Seeing beyond the technology.

While still in her corporate role, Valarie enjoyed orchestrating the delivery of customer-centric technology solutions. But over time, she found the team development aspect significantly more rewarding.

She shifted her attention from the technology itself to the people delivering it. "I enjoyed optimizing the performance and capability of the professionals in our global organization."

The Barrier Shift

Recognizing technical aptitude.

"Due to various circumstances, I realized that a lack of a traditional degree shouldn’t necessarily be a barrier to having a successful career. I felt that, while some jobs should require a university degree, some should not, given the wide access to knowledge and technology today."

The Catalyst

Scaling innovation and talent.

The turning point came when Valarie cultivated successful tech startup organizations, facilitating connections to large corporations. This experience piqued her interest in entrepreneurship and ecosystem building to bridge the gap between emerging talent and corporate innovation.

The Mission Today

Creating career mobility at all levels.

Valarie formed WrightNow Solutions to bridge the gap between technical aptitude and executive expectations.
